# Orbital Dawn — Home Visual Reference Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup

Asset type: production-quality visual reference for a full-viewport interactive Three.js personal portfolio homepage, 16:9 landscape desktop

Primary request: Create a highly polished cinematic sci-fi homepage keyframe titled “Orbital Dawn”, designed as an interactive movie poster rather than a conventional webpage.

Scene/backdrop: immense deep black outer space with three scales of stars; most stars are extremely dim pinpoints, a few medium cool-white stars, and only several restrained diffraction starbursts; a nearly invisible diagonal band of cosmic dust adds depth without becoming a colorful nebula.

Subject: Earth appears only as a huge curved horizon occupying the lower 30–36% of the frame. A concentrated orbital sunrise sits on the horizon around 60% from the left and 72% from the top: tiny white-hot core, warm amber/orange Mie scattering close to the horizon, thin electric cyan-blue upper atmosphere. On the right is a massive spherical futuristic orbital station occupying about 30% of the frame, partly cropped by the right edge, mostly in silhouette with intricate industrial panels, tiny warm cabin windows, a sharp cool rim light from the sunrise, and faint blue Earth bounce on its underside.

Style/medium: shippable high-end WebGL homepage mockup, photoreal cinematic 3D rendering, premium science-fiction production design, physically plausible materials and lighting, restrained and sophisticated, not fantasy illustration.

Composition/framing: wide 16:9, single dominant luminance focus at the sunrise; large calm negative space across the upper-left and center; strong depth from distant stars, Earth horizon, and near-field cropped station. Earth and station must not compete equally; sunrise is the absolute focal point.

Interface: very minimal borderless DOM-like typography floating in the negative space at upper-left. Small spaced cyan eyebrow reading exactly “K O B I N F L O W  ·  J I N  K O B I N”. Large two-line white sans-serif headline reading exactly “做能验收的 agent，\n不只更吵的对话。” Below it, a short muted gray description and one thin understated cyan interaction cue. A tiny four-item navigation sits near the lower-right but above the Earth, with no opaque panel, no glass card, no large rectangles. Keep all interface secondary to the cinematic scene.

Lighting/mood: awe, silence, scale, precision, orbital dawn; 80% deep shadow, 15% cool cyan atmospheric and rim light, 5% warm amber sunrise and windows.

Color palette: near-black #010306, deep navy #03111F, cold cyan-blue atmosphere, restrained white typography, warm amber/orange only at the sunrise and windows.

Materials/textures: Earth with convincing cloud depth, ocean specular response and subtle night-side city lights; station with weathered dark metal, varied roughness, panel seams, decals without readable branding, small imperfections.

Constraints: one full browser viewport only; no browser chrome; no scrolling page; no opaque HUD cards; no big orbital rings; no green aurora; no colorful nebula; no dense asteroid field; no excessive particles; no game HUD clutter; no lens flare covering the title; no watermark; no extra logos; no duplicate text; no additional spacecraft. The image must work as a concrete composition, lighting, palette and hierarchy reference for a real-time implementation.
